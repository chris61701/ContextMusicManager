    What to listen for. Sweep slowly across a station and back. A real receiver
    gives you hiss, then the music arriving dull and mono under that hiss, then treble and
    stereo opening up as you centre it — and the reverse on the way out. If the music arrives
    already bright and stereo and merely quiet, the high-cut and blend are doing nothing and it
    will sound like a volume fade, not a tuning. Between two close stations you should get one,
    a burst of noise, then the other, never a polite blend of both — that is
    capture, and at 3–4 dB it is a much harder edge than most people expect.
