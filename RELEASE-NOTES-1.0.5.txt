Context Music Manager 1.0.5

Everything below is measured against 1.0.4b.


NEW

- Music through in-world speakers. Radios, jukeboxes and speaker blocks play your Radio music
  where they physically stand, so a POI you are clearing has music coming out of the room
  instead of out of your head. Works on 2.6 and 3.x.
- A real acoustic model, not a distance fade. Sound routes through doorways and around corners
  rather than through walls, and reverb is graded by how much survives the bounces - a stone
  cave carries it, a wood room kills it.
- Personal radio on Alt+PgUp / Alt+PgDn, which walk Off - station - station - Off, so two keys
  give on, off, next and previous. Works on every supported version, alphas included.
- Radio stations are the subfolders of your Radio folder, each on a shared world clock, so
  everyone on a server hears the same song at the same moment and tuning in joins a broadcast
  already in progress.
- Changing station sounds like tuning a radio. The station you leave sinks under rising static
  rather than being cut, and the new one arrives distorted before opening out.
- Craft a shelf speaker or a ham radio, place it, then look at it and hold the activate key to
  pick its station from the game's own radial menu.
- A station with nothing playable sits on its own note rather than going silent, seeded from
  the station's name so a dead channel keeps its pitch on every client and across restarts.
- Music packs can ship as their own mods. CMM reads the Music folder of every installed mod as
  well as your own, so a soundtrack survives updating CMM and works with MO2, Vortex and
  Wabbajack.
- Playlist sources: a context can point at an .m3u or .m3u8 instead of a folder, so one
  playlist draws from anywhere on disk without moving or copying anything.
- Context HUD on Alt+Numpad9 - what is playing, why, and where it came from.
- Coverage panel on Alt+Numpad8 - which contexts have music, which are empty, and what each
  empty one falls back to.
- Now Playing overlay on Alt+Keypad7.
- Ban a track with Alt+Delete, undo with Alt+End, favourite with Alt+Insert, and cycle fight
  music with Alt+Home.
- Next and Prev walk the history of what you actually heard in that context, like a music
  player, instead of rolling a fresh random track.
- Per-section switches for vanilla Dynamic Music, so you can silence Combat or Suspense without
  turning the whole thing off.
- Console commands: cmm roots, cmm scan, cmm rotation, cmm acoustics, cmm emitters, cmm sfx.
- The radio dial has recorded sounds, in the mod's Sounds folder - swap them for anything.


CHANGED

- A big library finally sounds like one. Every track now plays before any track repeats; the
  old behaviour excluded exactly one track, so something could come back after three.
- Your config files are yours. settings.xml, radio.xml and contexts.xml are written on first
  run rather than shipped, so updating adds only what is new and leaves your settings alone.
  Previously every update reset everything you had chosen.
- The per-context Music folders are created by the mod where your music actually lives, not
  only inside the mod folder, and contexts you add yourself get folders too.
- World speakers follow the Ambient slider rather than Music, because a radio playing out of a
  block is an environmental sound.
- An infested POI now carries its ordinary tier music underneath it instead of excluding it.
- A huge music folder becomes playable long before the scan finishes.
- Favourites are scaled to folder size, so marking one means roughly the same thing in a
  library of fifty tracks and one of five thousand.
- CMM's keys go inert while the F1 console is open.


FIXED

- The game's Overall Volume slider now works on CMM's music. Turning the master to zero used
  to silence the whole game except this mod.
- Installing on a dedicated server no longer freezes joining players. CMM is client-side only
  and now detects a server and stays inert.
- FLAC files with an ID3 tag would not play at all. In one 9,839-file library, 146 were
  affected - and because tagging tools work an album at a time, they arrive in whole albums.
- Speakers stopped being detected, often for the rest of the session, because block ids are
  reassigned on every world load. The second world of a session went silent.
- Live edits went unnoticed on exFAT, FAT, SD cards and many network shares. Adding, removing
  or renaming tracks now registers there too.
- Music no longer starts at the wrong volume and slides into place a second later, and tracks
  that had not been measured yet no longer play 8-12 dB too loud.
- Long tracks started at the wrong volume and stayed there for their whole length.
- Death and spawn stingers were delayed by up to three seconds, waiting for a measurement that
  could never arrive.
- Radio music was quietly distorted. The receiver's drive stage never fully switched off, so
  every fully-tuned station carried 2-7% distortion.
- Starting a world speaker froze the game briefly - 403 ms in the worst case. Now under 9 ms.
- World speakers went quiet in the middle of a station change, and ticked when you turned your
  head near one.
- Tuning a speaker to a dead station gave static, a pause, then more static. It is one hiss
  now, settling onto the tone.
- Removing a station's music left it playing anyway, to the end of the song.
- Large video files stalled the decoder. A 2 GB mp4 took over 25 seconds to open and now takes
  0.7; a 20 GB mkv took about 130 seconds and now takes 0.2.
- The radio kept playing after you switched it off, and switching it on lost your place in the
  track underneath.
- A briefly disconnected drive could take a station off the air permanently, or leave a context
  looking empty for the rest of the session.
- Music could stop after a track ended if the next one had been deleted or moved, until you
  walked into a different context.
- Replacing a music file kept the old file's volume, and re-encoding a radio track put the
  station out of step for everyone.
- Leaving a world kept its acoustics in memory, up to about 43 MB.
- A hint of the main-menu theme could be heard while paused, and a track ending during a pause
  unmuted everything.
- Near a trader, the vanilla fallback could hold the wrong music for minutes, and with the
  trader disabled a fight played exploration music.
- Wrong or missing context music on Turkish and Azerbaijani system locales.
- On Alpha 20 and 21 the mod read the wrong game option, and in some cases failed to load at
  all.
